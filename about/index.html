About Us

Founded in 2014 Soraytec is a solution provider for electricity network operators. We provide innovative hardware solutions for primary side measurements and monitoring as well as software solutions to optimize efficiency, power quality, demand side management, forecasting, flexibility, grid automation, fault detection and fault localization.
